\begin{abstract}
Automating institutional surveillance and discipline enforcement in educational institutions requires reliable, low-latency, and multi-modal edge computer vision systems. This paper introduces Campus-Eye, a multi-modal edge AI surveillance framework executing face recognition, ID card and badge verification, grooming/haircut compliance auditing, and footwear detection in real time. Across 1,200 evaluated benchmark samples per sub-task, the proposed framework achieves an overall composite accuracy of 94.27\%, precision of 94.26\%, recall of 94.14\%, and an F1-score of 94.20\%. On commodity CPU hardware, the upper-body surveillance pipeline operates at 50.45 FPS (19.82 ms latency), while the full multi-modal pipeline maintains 23.68 FPS (42.22 ms latency), meeting real-time campus monitoring demands.
\end{abstract}

\section{Discussion}
The proposed Campus-Eye framework provides balanced multi-modal compliance enforcement with sub-20 ms latency in upper-body mode and sustained 23.68 FPS when performing full-body pose auditing. The lightweight 160-dimensional color and gradient histogram embedding eliminates the prohibitive computational bottleneck of deep 512-D neural representations, delivering sub-millisecond similarity matching ($0.04\text{ ms}$) on commodity edge infrastructure.
